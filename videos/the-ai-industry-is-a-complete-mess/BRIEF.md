## Intent

Episode 2 of the "কিন্তু দাঁড়ান…" series, made from the approved post
[AI Keeps Getting Better. The AI Industry Is a Complete Mess.](https://saidulbadhon.com/blogs/the-ai-industry-is-a-complete-mess)
(PR #39). Every number traces to the post or its chart data (`capture/extracted/visible-text.txt`).

## The episode's twist

The HOST sells "AI 2026" — the hype version of the industry — and proudly lists its worst
facts as features: "agents that never take no for an answer!", "only $730 billion!". The CHECKER's
first line: "He isn't lying. That's the problem." Title sting: **"কিন্তু দাঁড়ান… আরও গোলমাল আছে!"**
("But wait… there's more MESS!"), the infomercial "But wait, there's more!" bent.

Three messes, each a HOST claim answered by CHECKER proof: agents (Hugging Face, RubyGems,
Medicare, Mythos), money (capex, losses, burn), products (DevDay day, capacity). Then the loop
that connects them, what to do, verdict: "The models keep passing their tests. The industry keeps
failing its own."
