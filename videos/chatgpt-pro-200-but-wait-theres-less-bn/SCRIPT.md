# SCRIPT — chatgpt-pro-200-but-wait-theres-less-bn (বাংলা)

**Voice:** two voices, one per frame — HOST (frames 1, 4, 6, 9, 12, 14, 18) and CHECKER (frames 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15, 16, 17). Provider: Google Gemini TTS (`gemini-3.8-flash-tts`), language `bn`. Voice names and delivery styles live in `.hyperframes/voices.json`.
**Voice settings:** Gemini paces through the style prompt (speed fixed at 1).
**Voice direction:** HOST — a Bangladeshi late-night TV infomercial host: loud, grinning, every line an exclamation. CHECKER — dry, deadpan, unhurried; the calm adult who paused the tape. Register: everyday Bangladeshi Bangla, English kept for tech terms and product names, the way Bangladeshi tech YouTubers talk.

Frame 2 (the freeze) and frame 19 (the end card) are silent.
